  [ANSWER] Tripadvisor guidelines state that reviews submitted by property representatives, including owners, staff employees, or affiliated companies, are removed. Specifically, reviews from anyone who owns, is employed by, or is affiliated with the reviewed property are not allowed. Only TripAdvisor staff can remove reviews, but owners can report reviews that breach guidelines. In 2023, 40.35% of all reviews moderated by humans were removed, and less than 1% of reviews were contested by the community, with less than 10% of those removed for fraud.

  [ANSWER] According to the sources, Tripadvisor does not take lightly the decision to block or remove a review from its site, believing that genuine consumers should be able to share their experiences. Reviews are subjected to a rigorous moderation process before being made visible. While reviews can be rejected or removed for various reasons, including guideline violations and fraud, the platform does not delete reviews solely because they contain negative content. Reviews from business owners or employees are not automatically prohibited, but fake reviews, defined as those submitted by biased individuals or those without personal experience, are strictly against policy.
