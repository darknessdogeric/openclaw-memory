5.
对管理合同定义的增添和修订
ADDITION AND RIVISION TO DEFINITIONS IN THE MANAGEMENT CONTRACT
5.1
管理合同第1.1条中的“皇冠假日酒店标准"一术语应被删除并被替换为“品牌标准”，具体定义
如下：
In Clause 1.1 of the Management Contract, the definition of "Crowne Plaza Standards"
shall be deleted and substituted with "Brand Standards", the term of which is specifically
defined as follows:
“品牌标准"指IHG为品牌制定的,普遍适用于大中华地区品牌旗下经营的酒店的经营标准与产
品标准（包括消防标准、人身安全标准及设计准则），其可由IHG不时修改。
"Brand Standards" means the operational and product standards (including fire and life
safety standards and Design Guide) established by IHG for the Brand and applying generally
to hotels operated under the Brand in the Greater China region, which may be altered by
IHG from time to time.
