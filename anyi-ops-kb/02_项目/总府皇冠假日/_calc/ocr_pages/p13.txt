6.2
针对基本管理费的修订
Amendments to the Basic Management Fee
6.2.1
鉴于管理人及其关联公司在延期内向业主提供本协议第6.5条项下的知识产权权利许可，业
应向管理人或管理人指定的其他人支付一项许可费（“许可费"）。该许可费指相当于任一完
整的或不完整的月份中经调整总收入百分之二（2%）的费用。
In consideration of the IP Rights License provided by Manager and its Affiliates to Owner
under Clause 6.5 of this Agreement for the duration of the Extended Term, Owner shall,
Management Fee (which is payable to Manager during the original term of the Management
Contract) but shall, as a substitution for the Basic Management Fee, pay to Manager or other
person designated by Manager a License Fee. License Fee means an amount equal to two
percent (2%) of monthly Adjusted Gross Revenues in any complete or incomplete month.
6.2.2
许可费，应在其发生之月的次月的第十（10）日之前，按照第6.1条的规定，从银行账户中
支付。许可费应以人民币计算并支付给管理人。
In accordance with clause 6.1, the License Fee shall be paid no later than the tenth (10th)
day of the month following the month in which the License Fee was earned and shall be paid
from the Bank Account. The License Fee shall be calculated in Renminbi and paid to
Manager.
6.2.3
管理人应承担管理人就鼓励性管理费及许可费在中国应纳的到期应付的所得税税款。除前述
所得税以外的任何适用于管理人根据本协议向业主所收取的鼓励性管理费及许可费在中国的
税款或费用，例如间接税（如增值税）及附加税费等应由业主承担并支付。尽管有此约定，
但管理人不会要求业主承担因收取的鼓励性管理费及许可费而发生的、且以相关增值税税额
为基数计算的增值税附加。并管理人应按期向业主提供对应相应已支付款项的增值税专用发
票。
Manager shall bear income taxes which are properly due and payable by Manager in the
PRC in respect of the Incentive Management Fee and License Fee. Other than the aforesaid
income taxes, the Owner agrees to bear and pay any other taxes and charges whatsoever
due in PRC such as Indirect Taxes (such as Value Added Tax) and local levies in respect of
all the payments of Incentive Management Fee and License Fee. To avoid any doubts, the
foregoing taxes and charges that are borne by Owner are not included in any of the payments
pursuant to this Agreement and Owner shall pay and bear such amount in addition to all the
payments from Owner that Manager is entitled to pursuant to this Agreement.
Notwithstanding, Manager will not request Owner to bear the VAT Surcharges calculated
based on VAT in respect of the Incentive Management Fee and License Fees and Manager
shall provide relevant VAT invoices to Owner timely for the fees having been paid by Owner.
