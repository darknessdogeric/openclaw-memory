3.
管理合同转让
ASSIGNMENT OF THE MANAGEMENT CONTRACT
3.1
基于业主遵守本协议项下第1条约定的前提条件，业主与HICL及管理人同意，自2019年
09月28日（"转让日"）起且在整个延期内，HICL将其在管理合同项下的所有权利、收益、
财产权及利益转让给管理人，无论这些权利、收益、财产权及利益出现于转让日之前、之时
还是之后。
Subject to Owner's satisfaction of the pre-conditions set out in Clause 1 of this Agreement,
("Assignment Date") and throughout the Extended Term, HICL shall assign to Manager all
its rights, benefits, title and interest under the Management Contract, irrespective of whether
they arise before, on or after the Assignment Date.
3.2
基于业主遵守本协议项下第1条约定的前提条件，业主与HICL及管理人同意，自转让日起
且在整个延期内，管理人将承担HICL在管理合同项下的所有义务和责任，无论这些义务和
责任出现于转让日之前、之时还是之后。
Subject to Owner's satisfaction of the pre-conditions set out in Clause 1 of this Agreement,
Owner, HICL and Manager agree that, with effect on and from the Assignment Date and
throughout the Extended Term, Manager shall assume all the obligations and liabilities of
HICL under the Management Contract, irrespective of whether they arise or accrue before,
on or after the Assignment Date.
