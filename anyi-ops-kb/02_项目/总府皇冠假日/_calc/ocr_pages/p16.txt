(a)
收益管理
Revenue Management Services
在延期内，业主须在酒店聘用收益管理专员以管理酒店的价格、房态和预订渠道，
且该收益管理专员须通过 IHG考核并经认证（“酒店收益管理专员"）。如果业主未
聘用上述酒店收益管理专员，则业主应聘用管理人提供远程收益管理解决方案服务，
该服务包括由通过IHG考核并经认可的收益管理专员向酒店提供房态控制、价格制
定、预测和预订渠道管理服务（“收益管理服务"）。
During the Extended Term, Owner shall hire revenue management specialists at
the Hotel to manage the Hotel's pricing, inventory and channels and these revenue
management specialists shall be certified by IHG ("Hotel Revenue Management
Specialists"). If Owner does not hire the aforesaid Hotel Revenue Management
Specialists, Owner shall engage Manager to provide the off-site revenue
management solution services. Such services include inventory control, pricing
effectiveness, forecasting and channel management ("Revenue Management
Services") provided to the Hotel by IHG certified revenue management specialists.
