执执行摘要EXECUTIVE SUMMARY 
1.01 
该报告的目的是审阅成都总府皇冠假日酒店的当前状况，评估酒店转换为管理的全新一代
皇冠假日酒店运营的可能性。 
The objective of this report is to review the current conditions of the Crowne Plaza Chengdu 
Hotel to evaluate the Hotel’s continued operation as a Crowne Plaza (Managed) hotel. 
 
1.02 
成都总府皇冠酒店（402 间客房）开业22 年（1997 年开业）。 
The Crowne Plaza Chengdu Hotel (402 existing keys) is 22 years old (opened 1997).   
1.03 
自2011 年以来，酒店已经签署了许多短期管理协议。 PIP 写于（2011 年），大部分项目仍
未完成。 在过去几年中，对有限区域的部分进行了翻新（地毯更换等）。  Since 2011, the 
hotel has executed numerous short-term management agreements.  A PIP was written (2011) and 
a majority of items remain outstanding.  Partial refurbishment (carpet replacement, etc.) of 
limited areas has occurred for the past number of years. 
1.04 
现有酒店的设计和外观较为过时。 酒店的日常维护和保养达到平均标准，但正常的磨损对
酒店外观造成了影响。The hotel is dated in design and appearance.   Maintenance and upkeep 
of the hotel has been performed to an average standard, but normal wear and tear has had its 
toll on the hotel appearance.                                                                                                                               
1.05 
机械系统已接近其使用寿命的最后期限。 建议由合格的顾问对系统进行检查和测试。 顾问
应提供延长这些系统使用寿命的建议。The mechanical systems are approaching the last term 
of their service life.  Inspection and testing of the systems is advised to be performed by a 
qualified consultant.  The consultant should provide recommendations to extend the service life 
of these systems. 
1.06 
业主公司表达了在特定时间范围内翻新酒店的愿望。 翻新范围包括酒店的所有区域。制定
短期的策略计划。 
The Owning Company has expressed willingness to renovate the property within a given 
timeframe.  Improvements to include all areas of the hotel.  A short-term strategic plan is to be 
developed.                
1.07 
IHG 要求酒店进行设计和翻新，以符合最新的皇冠假日的品牌和设计标准。 
IHG requires the hotel be designed and renovated to comply with the latest Crowne Plaza brand 
and design standards. 
1.08 
业主公司同意采用洲际酒店集团认可的顾问公司和供应商的服务。 
The Owning Company agrees to engage the services of IHG Qualified Consultants and Vendors. 
1.09 
 
品牌安全：酒店通常符合IHG BSS 要求的某些修改和安装。酒店已完成最新风险审计
（2016 年）中确定的所有可行项目。 
Brand Safety: The hotel generally complies to IHG BSS with certain modifications and installations 
required.  The hotel has completed all actionable items identified in the latest Risk Audit (2016).
