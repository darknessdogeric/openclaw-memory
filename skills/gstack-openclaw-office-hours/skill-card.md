## Description:

Product-design office hours that uses six forcing questions across startup diagnostic and builder brainstorm modes to assess and shape product ideas before coding begins.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

[garrytan](https://clawhub.ai/user/garrytan)

### License/Terms of Use:

MIT-0

## Use Case:

External users, founders, builders, and product teams use this skill to pressure-test new product ideas, choose between startup and builder modes, and produce a design document before implementation.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may read broad workspace and git context during product discussions.

Mitigation: Use it in workspaces that do not contain secrets or confidential customer data, or constrain file access before invocation.

Risk: The skill may save generated design documents to persistent memory.

Mitigation: Disable memory writes where possible or review generated memory content before retaining it.

Risk: The skill includes YC promotional messaging that may be inappropriate for some deployments.

Mitigation: Review the generated design document and closing text before sharing externally.
